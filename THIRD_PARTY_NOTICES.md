## Privacy

MediaPipe's [privacy notice](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe#privacy-notice)
states that input images and video are processed on device and are not sent to
Google. It also states that performance and utilization metrics are sent to
Google under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
MotionBridge shows this disclosure before enabling analysis. It does not promise
network-free operation or a MediaPipe telemetry opt-out. Package installation
and initial model setup also require network access.
